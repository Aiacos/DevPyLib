Spring_Chain

A chain that trails behind a single lead ctrl with a cascading spring, for dragged ropes and dragon / snake bodies (after the Control Rig "dynamic chain"). Every joint is pulled by a spring towards where it would be if the chain was rigid from the joint before it, so the delay grows towards the tip. Bones keep their length.

Needs the DevPyLib plugin spring_chain_solver.py (folder mayaLib/plugin). The node loads it when it is built. Load the plugin before opening a saved rig (or add that folder to MAYA_PLUG_IN_PATH): if it is not loaded, the scene opens without any error, the solver nodes become unknown nodes and the chain collapses.

Guides

  • 0000 ... N – aim guides, each one aims at the next. 0000 is the lead end. They share one side pin, which sets the twist of the whole chain.

Ctrls

  • lead – drives the chain. The first two joints follow it rigidly, the others trail behind. Scale is uniform. Keyable attrs:
    • spring_weight – 0 = rigid chain, 1 = spring chain.
    • lag – frames of delay each joint adds on top of the joint before it. 0 = rigid.
    • damping – 1 = settles without overshooting, lower values bounce.

Attributes

  • Ctrls Scale – size of the lead ctrl.
  • Joints – 3 to 200. Needs a re-template.
  • Skinning Joints – tag the joints as skinning joints.
  • Lag Per Joint, Damping – default values of the lag and damping ctrl attrs.
  • Delayed Twist – the twist of the lead travels down the chain with the same delay. Unchecked, the twist is immediate.
  • Space – World delays any motion of the lead. Root Input does not delay the motion of what drives Root Input.

Connections

  • Root Input – space of the lead ctrl.
  • Root Output – first joint.
  • Tip Output – last joint.

How the simulation behaves

  • It is a simulation with state: play it forward frame by frame. Going back, or jumping more than one frame, resets the chain on its rigid pose, and it is correct again once played from there.
  • Dragging the lead on a paused frame makes the chain react.
  • Evaluating the same frame twice does not advance the simulation, so baking gives what playback shows.
  • Cached Playback is turned off while a Spring_Chain is in the scene (Maya shows the reason). A cache filled from the middle of the range would store wrong poses. Bake the joints to use it again.
  • Bake the joints (playing from a few frames before the shot) before sending a shot to a farm that renders frames out of order.
  • No gravity, no collisions, no FK ctrls along the chain. For a rope pinned at both ends use Dynamic_FK_Chain.
